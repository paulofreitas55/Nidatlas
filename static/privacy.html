Privacy

Nidatlas is a public bird atlas. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use it.

Photo identification (Identify)

When you use the Identify view, your photo is sent to our server only to run species identification against it. It's used solely for that purpose — never shared with anyone else, never used for anything beyond identifying the species in that one photo, and not kept once a result has been returned to you. No account, name, or other personal information is required to use it.

Using Nidatlas generally

No account or sign-in is needed for anything on this site. The only thing remembered about a visit is your chosen language (Portuguese, Spanish or English), which is stored in your own browser and never sent to us.
